Julia 1 autopilot · 1 player · insert coin

Jev Strike // Space Shooter

An AI decision model, Julia 1, flies this ship. About every 50 ms the game reports what each of the nine moves would lead to; the model picks the move, whether to fire, and whether to bomb, and the game just executes that choice.

Survive the waves, collect the drifting supply blocks, and watch the nav computer below to see what the model saw.

Pickups & weapons
W weapon upgrades: spread → rapid → splash → 8-missile swarm
B bomb charge: clears bullets and ships nearby
F escort jet (4 max): circles the ship, firing outward as it orbits
M missile pod: +1 homing missile in flight (8 max)
(·)Magnet pulls nearby pickups in; no need to touch them
Shield (rare): absorbs one hit, stacks to 4 (cyan · green · magenta · gold)
[o]Armored shots: enemy bullets take 2 hits and missiles 3 to destroy; bombs and the strike still clear them

Each wave is a 3-minute stage with a random mission (bonus score and a bomb). One assistance strike per 10 waves sweeps the whole field from bottom to top; the model calls it when the bullet field goes wild. Every 5th wave adds a life (5 max).
