Pilot the ship. Survive first: keep moving, keep escape routes open, keep distance from enemy ships, and drift toward center only when that gains open space. Right increases x, down increases y. All directions remain available. Shooting also launches homing missiles that thin out the enemies. You start with 3 bombs; each destroys every bullet and enemy ship near you, so save them for moments with no safe move. Collect floating supplies when a safe move reaches them: bomb pickups refill bombs (max 6) and weapon blocks widen your spread shot and add missiles (up to 6). The game executes your choice exactly and will NOT correct a colliding move.
